GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF FINANCE
DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE
CENTRAL BOARD OF INDIRECT TAXES AND CUSTOMS

Notification No. 17/2022 – Central Tax
New Delhi, the 1st August, 2022

G.S.R. 612(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-rule (4) of rule 48 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017, the Government, on the recommendations of the Council, hereby notifies:

In the notification No. 13/2020 – Central Tax, dated 21st March, 2020, for the words "twenty crore rupees", the words "ten crore rupees" shall be substituted with effect from the 1st day of October, 2022.

Para 2: Any registered person whose aggregate turnover in any preceding financial year from 2017-18 onwards exceeds ten crore rupees shall mandatorily generate e-invoices with effect from the 1st day of October, 2022.
