GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF FINANCE
DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE
CENTRAL BOARD OF INDIRECT TAXES AND CUSTOMS

Notification No. 01/2027 – Central Tax (Universal B2B E-Invoicing)
New Delhi, the 10th January, 2027

G.S.R. 101(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-rule (4) of rule 48 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017, the Government, on the recommendations of the Council, hereby notifies:

In the notification No. 13/2020 – Central Tax, dated 21st March, 2020, for the words "two crore rupees", the words "one crore rupees" shall be substituted with effect from the 1st day of April, 2027.

Para 2: Any registered person whose aggregate turnover in any preceding financial year from 2017-18 onwards exceeds one crore rupees must mandatorily issue e-invoices for all B2B supplies with effect from the 1st day of April, 2027.
